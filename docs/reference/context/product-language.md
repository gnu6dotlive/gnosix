# GNOSIX product language

A small glossary of resolved product distinctions.

## Physical and semantic surfaces

**Dock**:
The product surface offering current-session controls and access to
AppStack.

**AppStack**:
The application-list product region within the shared Dock/AppStack material
envelope. It retains its semantic identity when the outer physical envelope
expands or collapses.
_Avoid_: Launcher as the canonical product name

**Material envelope**:
A physical painted shell that may contain multiple semantic product regions.

## Configurable application access

**Application-list binding**:
A configured input gesture associated with an application-list action and its
selected provider. Replacing its provider does not replace Dock or AppStack.

**Default-application role**:
A user's functional application choice, such as web browser, independent of a
particular executable name or fallback priority list.
